## Complete PDF information base

```text
docs/information-base/
├── index.md
└── <one complete Markdown transcription per PDF>

assets/img/pdf-pages/
└── <source-document>/
    ├── page-001.webp
    ├── page-002.webp
    └── ...
```

The information-base Markdown preserves all machine-readable PDF text plus a facsimile of every PDF page, ensuring figures, screenshots, diagrams, annotations, and non-extractable visual content remain available.

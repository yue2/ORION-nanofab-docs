# Safety Training

> **WARNING**  
> O₂ monitor Local superuser notes place the O₂ monitor outside the HIM room, with a normal reading around 20–21%; evacuate personnel if N₂ leakage causes O₂ to fall below 20%.

Teach fire-alarm/gas-suppression response, LN₂ cryogenic hazards, electrical/high-voltage labels, EMO purpose, vacuum/chamber hazards, and the distinction between user stop/escalation and Superuser recovery.

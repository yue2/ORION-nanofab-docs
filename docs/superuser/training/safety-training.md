# Safety Training

Trainees must finish facility fire safety induction before requesting tool trainings.

Facility facts every trainee must know:

1. **O₂ monitoring** — the O₂ monitor sits on the door frame outside the HIM room; normal reading is 20–21%. If an N₂ leak drops O₂ below 20%, evacuate personnel immediately (HIM training, p. 1; see [O₂/N₂ Safety](../safety/oxygen-nitrogen.md)).
2. **Fire alarm and gas suppression** — the HIM lab uses a gas-based fire-suppression system. When the fire alarm sounds, a suppression gas will be released after a **60-second delay**. Do **not** attempt to fight the fire — evacuate immediately to the designated fire assembly point (HIM training, p. 1; see [Fire & Gas Suppression](../safety/fire-suppression.md)).
3. **Corridor extinguisher** — the extinguisher mounted on the corridor wall is for small office-area fires only, outside the HIM room; it is not for suppressing a fire in the HIM lab.

The trainer must also cover, with reference to the Safety and Superuser pages:

- LN₂ / cryogenic hazards and the Dewar labels (see [LN₂ System](../superuser/maintenance/ln2-system.md); ZEISS manual, printed p. 11 / PDF p. 16)
- Electrical / high-voltage labels and the EMO (emergency mains off) switches (see [High Voltage](../safety/high-voltage.md); ZEISS manual, printed p. 13 / PDF p. 18)
- Vacuum and chamber hazards (see [Vacuum Chamber](../safety/vacuum-chamber.md))
- The difference between user stop/escalation ("stop and contact a Superuser") and Superuser recovery actions (see [Emergency](../safety/emergency.md) and the User Guide "Warning Signals / When to Stop")

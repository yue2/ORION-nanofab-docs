# Training Session 1

*Agenda for the first hands-on session. Prereading: [Safety Training](../training/safety-training.md).*

1. **Basic microscopy and environmental stability** — why the HIM needs vacuum, stable gun temperature, and acoustic/mechanical isolation:
   - Vacuum regions: gun/column vs. chamber; base-pressure expectations (see [Reference: Vacuum System](../../reference/vacuum-system.md)).
   - Gun temperature and the LN₂ Dewar; why acoustic and table stability matter for imaging (see [LN₂ System](../maintenance/ln2-system.md)).
2. **Tool components and HIM working principle** — GFIS source, trimer, extraction/accelerating column, Lens 2, apertures, deflectors, ET detector, stage; He vs. Ne operation (see [System Overview](../../reference/system-overview.md), [GFIS Controls](../../reference/gfis-controls.md)).
3. **Pre-use checks** — complete before each session:
   - Review previous log entries and alerts.
   - Record gun, mid-column and chamber base pressures; gun temperature and Dewar bottom pressure; gas/CDA and LN₂ status.
   - Verify trimer and communications when appropriate (see [Routine Checks](../maintenance/routine-checks.md) and [Before You Start](../../user-guide/before-you-start.md)).
4. **ZEN GUI and status bars** — Operation Center, Vacuum Dashboard, Sample Management, GFIS/Gas Box panels, Dewar Control; how to read state indicators and alarms.
5. **Sample holder, mounting, height, tilt/rotation, loading/unloading** — holder handling with clean gloves; sample mounting and height limits (confirm the reconciled 6 mm limit with the Tool Owner before teaching); stage tilt −5° to +54° and rotation; the full load/unload sequence and its interlocks (see [Sample Loading & Unloading](../../user-guide/loading-unloading.md)).
6. **Hands-on transfer into the main chamber** — supervised practice of: Exchange → Transfer → rod insertion and secure coupling → visual confirmation via chamber camera that the holder is seated → full retraction with rod-retracted interlock verified. Emphasize the two critical failure modes: never retract before confirming the holder is seated/secure, and never force a tilted rod (see [Sample Loading & Unloading](../../user-guide/loading-unloading.md)).
    - **Trainer reminder:** dropping the sample stage/holder by mishandling the transfer rod forces a main-chamber vent and counts as **one unauthorized operation** toward the two-strikes access policy — emphasize this before hands-on practice (it is a common mishap).
    - **Trainer reminder:** No volatile substance is allowed in this tool — advise users during training, especially about organic samples. Loading an inappropriate volatile/organic sample counts as **one unapproved operation**
7. **Beam alignment, astigmatism correction, focus and imaging practice** — practice on the standard Au sample: reach rough focus (Lens 2), confirm aperture/gas/landing-energy set points, correct astigmatism with the stigmator knobs, and iterate focus/wobble/gun-shift. Verify trimer status before serious imaging (see [Starting a Session](../../user-guide/starting-session.md), [Imaging](../../user-guide/imaging.md)).

*End-of-session check:* trainee can demonstrate items 3–6; formal assessment is tracked in the [Competency Test](../training/competency-test.md).

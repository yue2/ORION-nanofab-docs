# Competency Test

**Duration:** ~30 minutes.
**Sample:** standard gold (Au) sample for all imaging items.
**Open book:** the User Guide may be brought along and consulted during the test.
**Pass rule:** the trainee must demonstrate every item below independently (minimal prompting). If the trainee does not qualify, schedule additional training before granting independent access.

The assessor marks each item Pass/Fail:

### 1. Safety precautions
- [ ] Identifies the key hazards and precautions from the safety briefing: O₂ monitoring (evacuate below 20%), fire alarm / gas-suppression response (abort vs. evacuate), LN₂/cryogenic handling, high-voltage/EMO, and chamber/detector collision risks (see [Safety Training](../training/safety-training.md), [Warning Signals / When to Stop](../../user-guide/warning-signals.md)).
- [ ] States the "stop and contact a Superuser" conditions and confirms they will not attempt them alone (e.g., chamber venting, HV work, pump or source recovery).

### 2. Load / unload samples
- [ ] Loads and unloads a sample holder through the full sequence: Exchange → Stage Ready → Transfer → rod coupling and seating verification → retraction with the rod-retracted interlock confirmed (see [Sample Loading & Unloading](../../user-guide/loading-unloading.md)).
- [ ] Demonstrates the two critical cautions: never retract the rod before confirming the holder is secured/seated, and never force a tilted rod.

### 3. Turn on the He beam
- [ ] Selects the standard Au sample, powers on the ET detector, and confirms Normal imaging mode.
- [ ] Sets the He aperture/gas, landing energy, and spot control; ramps up Lens 2 with the leakage-current check (≤0.5 µA) (see [Starting a Session](../../user-guide/starting-session.md)).

### 4. Focus on the Au sample
- [ ] Achieves a focused image on the standard Au sample, correcting astigmatism and beam shift/wobble as needed (see [Starting a Session](../../user-guide/starting-session.md), [Imaging](../../user-guide/imaging.md)).

### 5. Take an image and save it to the folder
- [ ] Sets scan parameters, grabs an image, and saves it to the designated data location (see [Imaging — Imaging your sample](../../user-guide/imaging.md)).

### 6. Turn off the beam
- [ ] Shuts down correctly: gas Off / GFIS Standby, Lens 2 ramped down and powered off (below 100 V), ET detector (and flood gun, if used) powered down; confirms the vacuum state (see [Ending a Session](../../user-guide/ending-session.md)).

### 7. Simple patterning operation (ZEN)
- [ ] Demonstrates a simple pattern with the ZEN Patterning tab: draws a shape, sets the dose and verifies the expected beam current, runs and stops the pattern (see [Patterning — ZEN patterning](../../user-guide/patterning.md)).

### 8. NPVE patterning (required only if the trainee will use NPVE for their future experiments)
- [ ] Verifies the server/communication connection, grabs a reference image, draws or imports a simple pattern, defines a stop condition, and runs it (see [Patterning — NPVE](../../user-guide/patterning.md); advanced details in the NPVE User Guide).

After all items pass, the trainee may receive independent (non-Superuser) tool access. Record the result, date, and inform lab managers for record keeping and door access.

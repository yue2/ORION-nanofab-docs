# Troubleshooting

Start here when the instrument shows an abnormal condition, fails to reach an expected state, loses communication, cannot produce a usable trimer or image, or requires an intrusive recovery. Select the page that most closely matches the observed symptom before taking corrective action; each page gives a check order and its end-state/escalation.

- **Main Chamber Vent** — intrusive chamber recovery when a sample holder is dropped or cannot be retrieved through the air lock.
- **Gun Overheat** — high-temperature recovery after LN₂ exhaustion (source bake-and-cool).
- **Vacuum Problems** — abnormal pressure or pump/valve states in the gun/column, chamber, or Dewar.
- **Communication / Firmware** — controllers not communicating with the UI; Query Nodes and firmware checks.
- **Trimer Problems** — expected trimer not found or lost.
- **Image Problems** — no valid image: detector, beam, and alignment checks.

# GFIS Recovery

The ZEISS outage procedure states that the GFIS gun/column requires a bake after loss of UHV; its standard recommendation is 100 h.

1. Verify GFIS state is Pumped No Ion Pump and home apertures.

1. Run Warm, Bake and Get Cold for the approved bake duration with LN₂ supply connected.

1. After bake, perform the recommended Light Anneal and Maintain BIV source build.

1. Verify gun pressure reaches the required range before helium flow/trimer formation.

1. Enable GFIS high-voltage elements, load the SFIM sample, form/check trimer and complete alignment.

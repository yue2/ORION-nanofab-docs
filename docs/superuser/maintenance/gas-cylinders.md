# Gas Cylinders

Local superuser notes distinguish compressed air for column valves and dry N₂ for load-lock purge.

1. Monitor local pressure indications and replace cylinders at the locally documented threshold.

1. Before disconnecting, place the relevant Dewar/table suspension state as documented locally.

1. Close/isolate valves, exchange the cylinder and renew sealing tape as required.

1. Perform the documented static pressure leak check before returning to service.

1. Re-check pressure over the following days for unexpected loss.

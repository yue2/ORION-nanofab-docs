# Trimer Formation

> **WARNING**  
> Maintenance procedure Only trained Superusers should perform source/trimer formation and high-voltage related recovery.

1. Use the standard Au sample, power ET detector and place GFIS in Standby.

1. Maintenance → GFIS → Form the source; run the documented source-build recipe.

1. Start trimer selection and observe in SFIM.

1. Adjust accelerator/extractor/Lens 1 according to the local maintenance record until the three-atom structure is resolved.

1. Determine BIV by measuring trimer intensity and tuning extractor for maximum intensity.

1. Mechanically align the source and set source distance.

1. Capture a final screenshot and record BIV/maintenance details.

See HIM trimer form.pdf and ZEISS manual §5.2.

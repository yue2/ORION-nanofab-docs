# Routine Checks

Maintain a weekly log of base pressures, gun temperature and Dewar pressure. The ZEISS manual notes that trend data can provide early warning of problems and help service personnel.

1. Review previous log entries and alerts.
2. Record gun, mid-column and chamber base pressures.
3. Record gun temperature and Dewar bottom pressure.
4. Inspect gas/CDA and LN₂ status. Place order in advance.
5. Verify trimer and basic communications when appropriate.

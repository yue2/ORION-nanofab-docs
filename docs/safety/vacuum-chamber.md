# Vacuum / Chamber Safety

## Recognition (all users)
- Never defeat interlocks, and never put your hands inside the chamber or air lock while pumps are running or the stage can move.
- The stage can **crash the sample into lens 2 or the ET detector** — especially with tall samples. Always watch the chamber camera during stage motion and respect the sample-height limit (ZEISS §4.4.1, printed p. 69 / PDF p. 74).
- **Crush hazard** from mechanical stage/clamp motion; **magnetic field** of ~20 mT at the chamber surface (pacemaker caution) — see the ZEISS hazard labels (printed pp. 10–11 / PDF pp. 15–16).
- Surfaces connected to the cryogenic system are **cold** and can cause frostbite (see O₂ / N₂ Safety).
- Routine sample changes go through the **air lock** (User Guide: Sample Loading & Unloading) — not by venting the chamber.

## Superuser actions
- **Venting or opening the main chamber** (e.g., intrusive holder recovery) is a Superuser-only procedure requiring controlled pump/valve sequencing — follow Main Chamber Vent exactly and do not improvise.
- Vacuum faults that need valve/pump intervention follow the Vacuum Problems procedure.

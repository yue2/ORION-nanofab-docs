# Safety Overview

Standalone safety reference for **all tool users**. Recognition and stop information is repeated in the User Guide and Training; these pages are the canonical source. **Actionable steps are for trained Superusers only** — if you are unsure whether a situation is safe, **stop and contact a Superuser**.

- **O₂ / N₂ Safety** — low-oxygen hazard from N₂/LN₂; monitor location and evacuation response.
- **Fire & Gas Suppression** — fire alarm response, the 60-second suppression delay, and the abort/release switches (switch operation is a Superuser action).
- **High Voltage** — high-voltage and EMO hazards, restricted-area rules (HV work is Superuser/Service only).
- **Vacuum / Chamber Safety** — chamber, cryogenic and mechanical hazards; interlock cautions for load/unload.
- **Emergency Procedures** — immediate actions for an emergency; escalation paths.

# High Voltage

### Recognition (all users)
- ZEISS labels identify **electric-shock** and **UPS live-voltage** hazards (live voltages remain inside even when main power is off) and warn that high-voltage currents **can be fatal** (ZEISS, printed pp. 11–13 / PDF pp. 16–18).
- **Do not** touch high-voltage cables on the column or open electrical enclosures. Protective cover panels may only be removed by trained ZEISS service engineers.
- The **EMO** (emergency mains off) removes power in an emergency — it is not a routine shutdown control. Know its location and use it only in an emergency.
- If you see a damaged cable, an exposed connection, or a burning smell, **do not touch anything — stop, and contact a Superuser.**

### Superuser actions
- HV cable handling is allowed **only** under the approved maintenance procedures: gun-overheat HVP isolation (disconnect and cover cable ends with gloves) and the GFIS bake (unplug accelerator/extractor/Lens 1 and wrap in clean Al foil). Follow lockout/site electrical-safety requirements, and enable/disable HV elements only via Service Machine → Power Distribution.

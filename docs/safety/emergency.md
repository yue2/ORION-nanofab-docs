# Emergency Procedures

## Immediate response (all users)
- **Fire or smoke:** follow the alarm response and evacuate — see Fire & Gas Suppression.
- **Low-oxygen alarm:** evacuate immediately — see O₂ / N₂ Safety.
- **Electrical emergency** (smoke, sparking, shock, fire involving electrical equipment): press the **EMO** to remove microscope power if you can do so safely, then call for help and evacuate as needed. Do not touch a person in contact with live electricity until power is removed.
- Know the **emergency phone number, fire assembly point, and first-aid kit location** from the building induction.

## EMO — emergency power removal
The **EMO** button is only for a real emergency requiring immediate removal of electrical power. EMO shutdown can cause **data loss and may damage turbo pumps** — never use it for a routine shutdown (routine shutdown is on the Planned Shutdown page; ZEISS, printed p. 13 / PDF p. 18).

## After an emergency shutdown
- Do **not** restart the tool yourself. Inform a Superuser and the facility contact, and follow the Power-Up / GFIS Recovery procedures before returning the tool to service.

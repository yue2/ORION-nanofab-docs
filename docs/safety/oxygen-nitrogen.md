# O₂ / N₂ Safety

> **WARNING**  
> Low oxygen Local notes: O₂ monitor outside HIM room, channel 1; normal reading about 20–21%. If N₂ leakage causes O₂ below 20%, evacuate personnel.

ZEISS also identifies cryogenic LN₂ cold-surface/frostbite hazards and requires applicable safety data sheets.

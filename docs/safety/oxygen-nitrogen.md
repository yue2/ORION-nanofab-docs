# O₂ / N₂ Safety

> **WARNING**  
> **Low oxygen:** an N₂ or LN₂ leak in an enclosed space displaces oxygen. The O₂ monitor is on the door frame **outside the HIM room** (channel 1); normal reading is about **20–21%**. If the reading falls **below 20%**, evacuate personnel immediately and do not re-enter until the alarm clears and the area is ventilated (HIM training, p. 1). Normal users: **evacuate and contact a Superuser — do not investigate the leak.**

### Recognition (all users)
- The tool uses **N₂** (gas cylinders and the LN₂ supply tank) in confined spaces; a leak may not be visible or audible.
- **Cryogenic surfaces** (LN₂ tank, Dewar lines) cause severe cold burns/frostbite on contact; follow institutional LN₂ handling rules, PPE, and the ZEISS safety labels/MSDS guidance (ZEISS §2.3, printed p. 14 / PDF p. 19).

### Superuser actions
- Identify the source of an N₂/LN₂ leak (backroom gas cylinders; external LN₂ tank and transfer lines) using the gas-cylinder and LN₂ pages, and isolate supply valves only if it is safe to do so.
- Confirm O₂ monitor health (channel 1, ~20–21%) and coordinate re-entry/ventilation with facilities.

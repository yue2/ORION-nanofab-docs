# Fire & Gas Suppression

**Building fire-safety induction** is required before attending tool training.

### Recognition and response (all users)
- The lab rooms have **two fire sensors each**. If the fire alarm sounds, look for smoke/fire:
  - **Fire or smoke confirmed:** evacuate immediately to the fire assembly point. Do **not** attempt to fight the fire — the room uses a gas-based suppression system.
  - **No fire or smoke (false alarm):** a suppression gas is released **60 seconds** after the alarm. At the corridor fire-alarm panel, press **abort** within those 60 seconds to reset the alarm and prevent the release.
- When the suppression gas discharges, do not re-enter until the system is confirmed safe by facilities/emergency response.

> **WARNING**  
> The exact fire assembly point is confirmed during the building induction (not stated in the source). The corridor panel also has a **release** switch; its intended use is not documented in the available source — do not use it without instruction.

# Fire & Gas Suppression

Local superuser notes state there are fire sensors in each room and instruct evacuation to the fire assembly point for a fire. They also describe a 60-second suppression-gas release delay and corridor abort/release switches.

> **WARNING**  
> Verify locally before publishing as controlled safety procedure Alarm/suppression operation is facility-specific.

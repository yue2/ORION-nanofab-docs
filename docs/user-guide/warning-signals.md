# Warning Signals / When to Stop

> **WARNING**  
> Stop and contact a Superuser 
> 
- Unexplained system errors
- Gun temperature above 95K 
- Insufficient LN₂
- Abnormal vacuum
- O₂/fire/facility alarms
- Unexpected mechanical or transfer behavior.
- Stage is not secured inside the main chamber but the transfer rod has been retracted.

Normal users should recognize abnormal states but should not attempt chamber venting, high-voltage work, pump recovery, source maintenance or power-outage recovery.

Accumulating TWO unapproved operations will result in suspending the usership. A new round of training is required to obtain access to the tool again.

# Sample Loading & Unloading

- Sample stage is stored inside the main chamber by default.

### Bring the holder out (unload)

1. In ZEN software, from the top menu buttons, select Sample Management to open the sample management window.
2. Select Exchange to move the stage to the defined load/Stage Ready position.
3. Once Stage Ready is reached (Stage ready indicator is on in the sample management window), select Transfer. The loading dock will start to pump. Wait for ~10 min for the loading dock vacuum goes to 1.5E-2 torr. The transfer door will automatically open.
4. Wear gloves for next steps when handling the air lock and sample stage.
5. Insert the transfer rod steadily, fully couple it to the holder, then screw clockwise the black handle at the end of the transfer rod to lock it to the holder. During holder transfer, visually verify through the air-lock window that the holder slides onto the stage; before pulling the rod back, ensure it is fully disengaged. **If the transfer rod is tilted when inserting, DO NOT lock it to the sample stage. Retract the rod fully then try again.**
6. Fully retract the transfer rod until Rod Retracted indicator is on. Verify the Rod Retracted interlock either on the loading dock LEDs or Sample Management window in ZEN.
7. Select Store to close the transfer door.

### Mount the sample

8. Select Vent , open the air lock. Screw the transfer rod counterclockwise to unmount the sample stage. Remove the sample stage to mount the sample. Photograph the holder position when useful for the Sample Map.
9. Slot the sample stage back to the holder, screw the transfer rod clockwise to lock the stage to the rod. Close the air lock and click Store. **Make sure the Rod Retracted indicator is on.** Pump will not start unless the rod is fully retracted.

### Return the holder (load)

10. Wait for the loading dock to pump to Store status. Then click Transfer, either on the loading dock button or in ZEN software.
11. When transfer door is open, push the transfer rod in. Make sure the sample stage is not tilted, and fully secured onto the holder in the main chamber. Unscrew the transfer rod, retract it fully, then press Store to close the transfer door. **If the stage is tilted or not secure on the holder, DO NOT unscrew the transfer rod. Retract fully then try again.**

> **NOTE**  
> Stage caution 
> Use the chamber camera and established height limits to avoid collisions with the column or detector.
> Sample height limit is 6 mm.

# Sample Loading & Unloading

Use Sample Management to exchange the holder through the air lock.

1. Select Exchange .

1. If the holder is in the chamber, select Transfer , insert the transfer rod steadily, fully couple it to the holder, then retract.

1. Keep the transfer rod fully retracted before Store ; verify the Rod Retracted interlock.

1. Select Vent , open the load lock and mount the sample. Photograph the holder position when useful for the Sample Map.

1. Close the load lock and return to Store . Wait for the load chamber to pump before Transfer.

> **NOTE**  
> Stage caution Use the chamber camera and established height limits to avoid collisions with the column or detector.

## Logic review — item requiring confirmation

> **WARNING — WAITING FOR MANUAL CONFIRMATION**  
> **ZEISS manual guidance to confirm locally:** use **Exchange** to move the stage to the defined load/Stage Ready position; **Transfer** is used only once Stage Ready is reached. The Rod Retracted interlock inhibits gate-valve operations unless the rod is fully retracted. During holder transfer, visually verify through the air-lock window that the holder slides onto the stage; before pulling the rod back, ensure it is fully disengaged. For any subsequent stage motion, use the chamber scope to maintain clearance from Lens 2 and the ET detector.  
> **Manual references:** Sample Management controls, printed pp. **55–56** (PDF pages **60–61**); Loading the Sample, printed pp. **61–64** (PDF **66–69**); Stage Motion Risks §4.4.1, printed p. **69** (PDF **74**).  
> **Waiting for confirmation:** the Operator Manual does not establish the local numeric transfer value/calibration recorded in the site SOP. Confirm the current site-specific Z/clearance value before publishing a numeric limit.

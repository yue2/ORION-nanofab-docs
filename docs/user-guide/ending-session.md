# Ending a Session

1. Return tilt and scan rotation to 0°.
2. **Good Practice**: Return to the Au std sample position, grab a frame to confirm that the standard sample is in focus.
3. In GFIS settings, turn off gas. Make sure gas is Off / GFIS is in Standby.
4. Ramp down Lens 2 voltage. When voltage is below 100 V, uncheck the Lens 2 power.
5. Power down the ET detector. Power down flood gun if it was used.
6. When gas evacuation is finished, check the vacuum screen. The Gun to Main Chamber valve should be closed.
7. Select Exchange to move to the transfer position.
8. Unload the sample: with the gate open, push the transfer rod in and thread it clockwise into the holder until fully secured, then pull the holder out into the air lock. Close the gate, vent the air lock, open the door, and detach the rod counter-clockwise to remove your sample stub (detailed steps: [Sample Loading & Unloading](./loading-unloading.md); ZEISS §4.3.8).
9. If the session is fully over, leave the (empty) sample stage in the main chamber. Close the air lock door -> press Store to pump the air lock -> press Transfer to open the gate, push the platform back in and verify it seats on the stage, then disengage the rod fully before retracting it. -> press Store to close the transfer door.
10. Complete the logbook, clean the work area and transfer data using email.

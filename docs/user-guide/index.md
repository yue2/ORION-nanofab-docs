# User Guide

Normal-operation procedures for trained users. Read the pages in order on your first session; after that, jump to the task you need. If a situation is not covered here, **stop and contact a Superuser**.

- **Before You Start** — pre-use checks, stop conditions and the lab rules for a work session.
- **Sample Loading & Unloading** — transferring samples through the air lock; holder mounting, height and interlock cautions.
- **Starting a Session** — power-up sequence, He beam set-up, rough focus and trimer status check.
- **Imaging** — focus, beam shift/astigmatism correction, image capture, and flood-gun use.
- **Patterning** — simple ZEN patterning and advanced NPVE patterning.
- **Working with Neon** — switching from He to Ne and back.
- **Ending a Session** — shutdown sequence and data transfer rules.
- **Warning Signals / When to Stop** — when to stop and escalate, and the local access-policy rules.

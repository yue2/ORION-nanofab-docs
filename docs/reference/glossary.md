# Glossary

- **GFIS:** Gas Field Ion Source; helium or neon in the ORION NanoFab.

- **BIV:** Best Imaging Voltage, determined during source/trimer maintenance.

- **NPVE:** NanoPatterning and Visualization Engine.

- **Landing Energy:** Energy at which ions hit the sample, determined by accelerator voltage.

- **Lens 1:** Upper/condenser lens.

- **Lens 2:** Lower/objective lens focusing the beam on the sample.
